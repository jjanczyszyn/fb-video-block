# FB Video Block — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-08-13*

FB Video Block does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

- The extension runs entirely in your browser. It makes **no network requests** of any kind.
- It has **no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party services**.
- The only data it stores is your three on/off preference toggles, kept in
  Chrome's built-in extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`) so they follow
  your own Chrome profile. This data never leaves Google's Chrome sync
  infrastructure and is never visible to the extension's author.
- The extension only runs on `facebook.com` pages. It does not read, record,
  or transmit anything you see or do there — it only hides video content
  locally on your screen.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/jjanczyszyn/fb-video-block/issues
